# Northwind: business rules for sign-off

Engagement `northwind-demo` (Public demo: Microsoft Northwind 2.0 Developer Edition (Access)), spec version 1.0. `analysis/rules.json` is the source of truth and this page is its reader's copy for sign-off; `analysis/keep-drop.md` lists every design object. Statements are paraphrased from the scrubbed design: no code, query text, macro text or data values are copied. This is a public demo engagement, so the sign-off is simulated.

## Summary

Northwind is an order-management application for a small trading company: it takes customer orders, invoices and ships them, keeps stock levels and restocks through purchase orders, and prints sales reports and a product catalog. The new web application keeps four areas, each built as one feature module:

The foundation, built once in S4 and not an area, carries sign-in (local accounts), roles mirroring the application's privileges, and the audit fields every table carries. The spec also holds 13 code lists, 26 relations, 4 workflows (one per must-keep item) and 28 services, described target-neutrally in `analysis/interfaces.md`.

## Glossary

- **Order**: a customer order with one line per product; it moves New → Invoiced → Shipped → Paid → Closed.
- **Order line status**: New, Allocated (stock reserved), No Stock, On Order (covered by an approved purchase order), Invoiced, Shipped.
- **Invoice**: the printable document for one order; its number is the order number.
- **Trading company**: the company whose type marks it as the business itself; its address prints on the invoice and the catalog order form.
- **Tax status**: Taxable or Tax Exempt; tax is charged on the line subtotal of Taxable orders only, never on shipping.
- **Purchase order**: an order to a vendor; it moves New → Submitted → Approved → Received → Closed.
- **Stock take**: a counted quantity on hand for a product at a moment in time; the starting point of its stock figures.
- **Physical stock**: the last stock take plus purchase order lines received since, minus order lines invoiced since.
- **Allocated / no-stock / on-order quantity**: order line quantities in the Allocated or No Stock status, and purchase order line quantities of Approved purchase orders.
- **Quantity to sell**: physical stock minus allocated quantity.
- **Suggested reorder quantity**: the shortfall of supply against demand and the product's target level, at least the minimum reorder quantity.
- **Privilege / role**: a right granted to an employee, such as approving purchase orders; the new app's roles mirror them.
- **Area**: a functional part of the application; each kept area becomes one feature module of the new app.
- **Rule**: one paraphrased business behaviour with an id R-<area>-NNN, a trigger and the design objects it comes from (sources).
- **Trigger**: the event that runs the behaviour: a screen or report event, a query, a data macro, a procedure or a schedule.
- **Test kind**: how the rule is proven: `rule` (one automated rule test), `equivalence` (the new app's output equals the recorded output of the old one), `e2e` (an end-to-end test of a workflow).
- **Keep / drop / out of scope**: the decision for every design object; a kept object names the target component that implements it.
- **Target component**: where a kept object lands: `<area>.<part>` (views, services, saved queries, reports, jobs), `legacy.models` (the moved tables) or `foundation:<item>`.
- **Foundation**: what every area relies on, built once in S4: sign-in, roles mirroring the privileges, and audit fields.

## Workflows

### `order_to_ship`: Order entry to invoice (printable PDF) to shipping

- Forms: frmOrderList, frmOrderDetails, sfrmOrderLineItems
- Reports: rptInvoice
- End-to-end rules: R-orders-050, R-orders-029, R-orders-030
- Other rules it walks through: R-orders-001, R-orders-009, R-orders-015, R-orders-016, R-orders-017, R-orders-018, R-orders-020

1. sign in and land on the order list
2. start a new order and choose the customer
3. save the order header
4. add order lines (each line is allocated or marked no stock)
5. create the invoice and download it as a PDF
6. enter shipped date, shipper and shipping fee and ship the order
7. enter payment method and paid date and receive payment
8. close the order

### `inventory_restock`: Inventory levels and restocking

- Forms: frmProductList, frmProductDetail, sfrmProductDetail_StockTake, sfrmProductDetail_Vendors, sfrmProductDetail_PurchaseOrders, frmSelectVendorDialog, frmPurchaseOrderDetails
- Reports: none
- End-to-end rules: R-inventory-030, R-inventory-010, R-purchasing-023
- Other rules it walks through: R-inventory-001, R-inventory-006, R-inventory-008, R-inventory-009, R-inventory-011, R-inventory-017, R-inventory-018, R-inventory-019, R-inventory-020, R-inventory-021, R-inventory-022, R-purchasing-018

1. open a product's stock takes and enter a counted quantity
2. when the count differs from the expected quantity, confirm it
3. see the stock take saved with the system date and the expected quantity, and the product's figures and order line allocations updated
4. open the product list and read each product's stock figures
5. open a product whose quantity to sell is below its target level and check its suggested reorder quantity
6. reorder it, picking the vendor in the dialog when the product has several vendors
7. see the product added to the vendor's oldest New purchase order, or a new one, on the product's purchase-order history
8. after that purchase order is approved and received, see the physical stock rise and waiting order lines allocated

### `purchase_order_to_stock`: Purchase order to receiving to stock update

- Forms: frmPurchaseOrderList, frmPurchaseOrderDetails, sfrmPurchaseOrderLineItems
- Reports: none
- End-to-end rules: R-purchasing-003, R-purchasing-008
- Other rules it walks through: R-purchasing-001, R-purchasing-004, R-purchasing-005, R-purchasing-006, R-purchasing-007, R-purchasing-009, R-purchasing-010, R-purchasing-012, R-purchasing-019

1. open the purchase order list and start a new purchase order
2. choose the vendor and save the purchase order (status New)
3. add line items for the vendor's products, or add all no-stock products at once
4. submit the purchase order
5. approve it as a user with the approve-purchase-order privilege (its quantities now count as on order)
6. receive it after confirming (status Received; the received quantities raise available stock)
7. check the product's available stock on the product page
8. enter the shipping fee and payment method and close it (tax, payment amount and payment date stored)

### `kept_reports`: The invoice and the four chosen reports

- Forms: frmOrderDetails, frmReports
- Reports: rptInvoice, rptSalesByEmployee, rptSalesByProduct, rptSalesByProductQuarterly, rptProductCatalog, srptCatalog_TableOfContents, srptQuality, srptGastronomic, srptOrderForm, srptShipVia
- End-to-end rules: R-reports-016, R-reports-013, R-reports-014
- Other rules it walks through: R-orders-019, R-orders-020, R-orders-021, R-reports-001, R-reports-002, R-reports-007, R-reports-008, R-reports-009, R-reports-010, R-reports-012

1. open an invoiced order and open its invoice again as a PDF
2. open Reports from the main menu and see monthly sales per employee preselected with the default period
3. change the start and end dates, preview the report and filter it to one salesperson
4. switch to monthly and to quarterly sales per product and preview each
5. save a sales report as PDF
6. choose the product catalog, see the date inputs hidden, and generate it
7. check the table of contents, the fixed pages and the order form with the shipper list
8. save the catalog as PDF

## Assumptions

- There is no answers.md yet, so each open question carries the default stated in its text, and the rules describe the current behaviour until the client answers. An answer that changes a rule after signature is a change order.
- Lookup codes come from the design (code constants, value lists and query criteria), never from data. No lookup needs codes from the client, so no `values` are listed.
- All 20 BeforeChange data macros do the same audit stamping (created-by and created-on, modified-by and modified-on, with the created values protected). They are kept with one target component, `foundation:audit-fields`, built in S4 with sign-in and roles, and proven by R-orders-031, R-inventory-028 and R-purchasing-027.
- Sign-in (frmLogin, frmCredentials and the login query) moves to the foundation as local sign-in; the privileges tables and screen become roles that mirror the app's privileges (`foundation:roles`). Neither is an area.
- The table Strings is kept (`legacy.models`) although the orders area had dropped it: the product catalog's quality and gastronomic pages take their texts from it (R-reports-014). Its message-box texts are not used by the new app.
- The start-up macro's first-run date shift is kept as a one-off start-up job guarded by the first-run flag (R-orders-049, Q-orders-005).
- Besides the invoice, the four kept reports are monthly sales per employee, monthly sales per product, quarterly sales per product and the product catalog with its five sub-reports (Q-reports-001). The employee email and phone lists are dropped.
- Behaviour that looks like a defect is kept as it is until the client answers: the sales reports' end date at midnight (Q-reports-002), the vendor tab that hides unsubmitted purchase orders (Q-purchasing-002), the employee's recent orders taken from the 20 latest overall (R-orders-027, Q-orders-004).
- R-orders-027 states the current behaviour of the recent orders list so that its equivalence unit proves the statement. The orders area file had worded it as the proposed change (the employee's own 20 most recent) while citing only the query that computes the current behaviour; the merged spec follows the rule above, and Q-orders-004's default now matches it.
- EmployeePrivileges.EmployeeID is PROTECT although the Access relationship cascades, because the employee screen refuses to delete an employee who holds privileges (R-orders-040). Contacts.CompanyID is CASCADE although the relationship does not cascade, because deleting a company removes its contacts first (R-orders-033).
- Dates keep their time of day where the original stores one (order, invoice, stock take and purchase order dates); Q-purchasing-006 asks whether purchase order dates should be dates only.
- Access-only plumbing is dropped: ribbon callbacks, the recently-used list, window bookkeeping, requery and refresh handlers, message dialogs, the error log, the demo-data admin tools, and the welcome, help, about and learning screens (Q-orders-008).
- The app runs on PostgreSQL and is verified on SQL Server; it has local sign-in and no hosting in this demo (the public repository is the deliverable).
